HOW TO PLAY

DRIVE

Drag anywhere on the screen to steer. The car accelerates on its own, and there is no speed limit: speed keeps building while your line is clean, and turning is what costs you speed, so carve gently and save the hard turns for when you mean them. The round HB button is the only brake there is, a handbrake for tight turns and hard stops. Only a press that starts on the button itself brakes; a steering drag that crosses it does nothing.

FLY

The moment you leave the ground, that same drag becomes a flight stick. Drag down for a backflip, up for a front flip, sideways to roll, and diagonal for combos. Hold a second finger down to stabilize: your rotation settles, the car glides, and you can set it down level.

SCORE

Points stay pending while you are in the air and only bank when you land. Clean landings bank in full and grow the combo. A bad landing loses the whole air, but the car always rights itself and keeps rolling. Nothing stops you. Coins pay 250 each. Take off again within 3 seconds of landing to keep the combo alive.

THE VALLEY: one endless bowl, walls on both sides. Ride up one wall, launch off the lip, land on the far wall, and let every crossing throw you higher. The gold coins trace the riding lines. In the last minute the walls steepen, and the same lines throw you further. Over the rim is open sky and a long fall: you lose the air, and the run puts you back on the valley floor. Three minutes. Spend the height.
